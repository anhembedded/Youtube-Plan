# Vocabulary

One spelling and one meaning per term. Project-management terms that are not specific to this project are in the [Project-Process glossary](../Project-Process/Glossary.md).

## 1. Channel and content
| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Source story** | A story found on Chinese social media that a video is based on. Used only as an idea (Clarify C1). |
| **Remake** | A new video built from a source story's idea, with an original script, an AI voice and original or licensed visuals. Not a translation or re-upload. |
| **Niche** | The kind of story the channel publishes (genre), for one audience. |
| **Long video** | A video of several minutes or more, published on the channel's main feed. |
| **Shorts** | YouTube's vertical short videos. |
| **AI voice** | Narration generated by a text-to-speech tool. |

## 2. YouTube terms
The rules behind these terms are stated, with sources and dates, in Clarify C5. Copyright claim, strike, CTR, average view duration and RPM are working definitions until Discover checks them against YouTube's pages.
| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **YouTube Partner Program (YPP)** | YouTube's program that pays channels a share of revenue once they meet its thresholds and pass review (SRC-3). The ad-revenue tier and the lower fan-funding tier have different thresholds (SRC-9). |
| **Fan-funding tier** | The lower tier of the Partner Program: memberships, Super Chat, Super Thanks and shopping, but no ad revenue (SRC-9). |
| **Qualified watch hours** | Hours watched on public long-form videos; Shorts, private, unlisted, deleted and ad-campaign views do not count (SRC-3). |
| **Reused content** | YouTube's term for content repurposed from YouTube or another source without significant original commentary or change, including readings of material the channel did not create; not monetized (SRC-5). |
| **Inauthentic content** | YouTube's term (since 2025-07-15, formerly "repetitious content") for mass-produced or repetitive content, including AI content from generic templates; not monetized (SRC-5). |
| **Copyright claim** | A claim by a rights holder on a video, which can block it or move its revenue. |
| **Strike** | A penalty on a channel; repeated strikes can end the channel. |
| **CTR (click-through rate)** | Share of viewers who click a video after seeing its thumbnail, in percent. |
| **Average view duration** | Average time viewers watch a video, in minutes and seconds. |
| **RPM (revenue per mille)** | The channel's income per 1,000 views, in USD. |

## 3. Project terms
| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The owners** | The owner and the partner, who approve together (Clarify C7). "The owner" alone means SH-1. See [Stakeholders](../Project-Management/Stakeholders.md). |
| **Gate** | A checkpoint at the end of a step (Clarify, Discover, De-risk, Plan, Implement, Validate). See [Approach §5](../Project-Management/Initiation-phase/Approach.md#5-gates-and-exit-criteria). |
| **Growth model** | The chain of signals by which a video reaches viewers: impressions → click-through rate → watch time and retention → recommendations → subscribers and watch hours. Each link is a hypothesis until measured on the channel (Clarify G7). |
| **Source register** | The list of every source a fact comes from (SRC-n), in the [index](../README.md#7-source-register). |
| **Assumption log** | The list of every assumption (A-n) and its status, in the [index](../README.md#6-assumption-log). |
| **Pilot video** | The first video made from story to upload, to measure real hours, cost and quality before a batch. |
| **Spike** | A time-boxed experiment that answers one question (S-n). |
| **Grey area** | An open choice in Clarify (G-n), settled later by evidence. |
| **Epic** | A group of tasks with one measurable outcome (EPIC-nnn). |
| **Proposal** | A request for new scope that waits for the owner's yes or no (PRO-nnn). |
