## 1. Problem

### 1.1 Background
The owner and a partner want to start a new YouTube channel to earn money (Fact, SRC-1: "Me + a partner", "Equal partner, both approve", "Start from zero"). The content is stories found on Chinese social media, remade in the owners' own words and voiced by an AI voice (Fact, SRC-1). Neither owner reads Chinese, so every story is read through translation (Fact, SRC-1: "No, translation needed"). This repository manages the project: plans, decisions, research and tracking. It holds no videos (Fact, SRC-1). How the work is organised is set out in [PRO-001](../../../../Tasks/proposal/PRO-001.md).

## 4. Scope and grey areas
**In scope:**
- Choosing the channel's genre, language, audience, format and tools, on evidence.
- Creating the new channel and the production workflow.
- Producing and publishing videos until the channel is accepted into the Partner Program.
- Managing the project in this repository.

**Out of scope:**
- Re-uploading, translating or lightly editing someone else's video (C1).
- Storing videos, audio or project files of the videos in this repository (C6); they live in the owners' own storage.
- Growing the income after monetization. That is ongoing operation, which starts when this project ends.

**C5 in detail** (YouTube's official pages, all checked 2026-10-30; re-checked at every gate because the rules change):
- **a. Thresholds.** Ad revenue today needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days (Fact, SRC-3). For channels applying for the first time from 2027-02-01, the bar is 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days; channels already in the program keep their status (Fact, SRC-4). A lower tier of 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days and 3,000 watch hours (or 3 million Shorts views) unlocks fan funding and shopping but no ad revenue, in some countries (Fact, SRC-9). Meeting a threshold does not guarantee acceptance; review takes about a month (Fact, SRC-3).
- **b. Reused content.** Not monetized: content repurposed from YouTube or another source without significant original commentary or change, including *"content that exclusively features readings of other materials you did not originally create"* and *"short videos you compiled from other social media websites"*; this applies *"even if you have permission from the original creator"* (Fact, SRC-5). This bears directly on how far a remake must depart from its source story (A1, R-1).
- **c. Inauthentic content** (renamed from "repetitious content" on 2025-07-15). Not monetized: repetitive or mass-produced content, including *"AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production"* (Fact, SRC-5).
- **d. AI disclosure.** Realistic AI-made content must be disclosed at upload; non-realistic content (animation, fantasy) and AI help with scripts, titles or thumbnails need no disclosure; repeated failure to disclose can bring removal or suspension from the program (Fact, SRC-6). Whether an AI narration voice must be disclosed is not stated (Open question, Q-009).
