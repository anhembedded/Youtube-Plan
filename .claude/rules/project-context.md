# Project Context

Informal background so that an AI assistant understands the situation. It is **not** the project charter.
Scope, constraints, assumptions, open questions and acceptance criteria live in
[Clarify](../../Docs/Project-Management/Initiation-phase/Clarify/Clarify.md) and [Approach](../../Docs/Project-Management/Initiation-phase/Approach.md). If this file conflicts with them, they win.
State only what the owners confirmed; do not fill gaps with assumptions.

## Who and where

- The owner (GitHub `anhembedded`, the user in chat) runs the project with a partner. The partner is an equal partner: both approve every gate and every Tier C item (Clarify C7). The partner does not take part in the chat; how the partner's approval is recorded is open (Q-010).
- This repository is used for project management of the channel: plans, decisions, research and checklists. It holds no videos.
- The owner's method is in [Docs/Project-Process](../../Docs/Project-Process/Thinking%20New%20project.md) (Clarify, Discover, De-risk, Plan, Implement, Validate).

## Why the project exists

- The owners want to make money with a new YouTube channel, starting from zero.
- Content: stories found on Chinese social media, remade with the owners' own script and an AI voice. Re-uploading or lightly editing the original videos was ruled out. Neither owner reads Chinese: every story goes through translation.
- The project ends when the channel is in the Partner Program and the workflow is written and repeatable; income growth after that is ongoing operation.

## Not decided yet

- Story genre, video language and target audience, format, tools: from Discover and De-risk.
- Budget per month, hours per week, deadline: the owners decide, at the latest at the Plan gate.
